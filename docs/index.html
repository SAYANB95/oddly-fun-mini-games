Oddly Fun Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

What the game stores

Oddly Fun stores scores, coins, streaks and game progress locally on your device. The game does not require an account, and Knitlly Technology does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Advertising and data

The game uses Google Mobile Ads. Google and its partners may process device or other identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, diagnostics and approximate location derived from network information to provide, measure and protect advertising. Advertising is requested as non-personalized by default.

For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

Your choices

  • You can reset locally stored progress by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.
  • You can manage advertising controls through your Android and Google account settings.
  • The core games do not require an account or precise location access.

Children and changes

Oddly Fun is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We will update this policy before release if the game’s data practices materially change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact displayed on the Oddly Fun Google Play listing.

# Privacy Policy for Oddly Fun: Mini Games

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Oddly Fun stores game progress, scores, coins and streak information locally on
the user’s device. The app does not require an account and Knitlly Technology
does not directly collect names, email addresses, contacts or precise location.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads. Google and its partners may process device or
other identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, diagnostics and
approximate location derived from network information to provide, measure and
protect advertising. Advertising is requested as non-personalized by default.
Google’s handling of this information is governed by Google’s privacy policies.

Users can reset locally stored progress by clearing the app’s storage or
uninstalling the app. Questions may be sent to the developer contact shown on
the Google Play listing.

This policy will be updated before release if the app’s data practices change.
